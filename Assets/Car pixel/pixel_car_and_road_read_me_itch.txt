About
-----
Create a tiny top-down traffic game with this simple asset set.


Contents
--------
8 different colored/themed cars in 16 directions at 24 x 24 pixels.
4 different colored boats in 8 directions at 24 x 24 pixels.
Train engine and carriage in 16 directions at 24 x 24 pixels.
35 road tiles at 64 x 64 pixels in 5 different background colors (rotations included): straight, bend, ​4 way junction x2, traffic island, T-junction, pedestrian crossing, parking, start/finish line, dirt-crossover, blank.
4 road tiles at 128 x 128 pixels in 5 different background colours (bends only).
15 rail tiles at 64 x 64 pixels in 5 different background colors: staight, bend, t-junction, 4 way junction, stops. Frame 16 is a tilemap (16 x 16 pixels).
36 water and bridge tiles at 64 x 64 pixels.
1 set of traffic lights at  8 x 24 pixels.
139 scenery items of various sizes.
4 sets of animated walking people in 4 directions
1 set of animated zombies in 4 directions.
1 sheep animated in 8 directions + sitting frames.
1 shark animated in 8 directions.
1 bird animated in 8 directions.
2 cows static in 4 directions.
1 animated exhaust fx.
1 animated water fx.


Format
------
All in png format and sharing one 16-colour (PICO) palette.
Seperate sprites and spritesheets.
Aseprite format files for the above.
